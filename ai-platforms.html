Where Can I Access AI?

Find named platforms and official access links. Choose a service only after deciding whether AI adds value and checking your assessment rules and privacy requirements.

Product information last checked: 17 September 2026. Features and account arrangements can change; follow provider links for current details.

Check Access and Account Arrangements

Start with your institution's current guidance and any approved access it provides. Consumer accounts, work or school accounts and paid plans can have different features and data arrangements. A subscription does not itself establish privacy, accuracy or assessment permission.

Before paying or uploading material, check current provider information about pricing, availability, limits, storage, review, training and sharing, alongside institutional requirements. Do not assume an account includes integration with other applications.

Follow assessment permission, disclosure and privacy guidance. For tasks and specialist services, see the Toolkit; for checking outputs, see verification guidance.
